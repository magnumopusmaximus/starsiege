STAR SIEGE – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Who we are

Star Siege is developed by NovaByte Studio, Maxim Glockenhammer, Humboldtring 6, 14473 Potsdam, Germany. Contact: novabytestudio.team@gmail.com.

Summary

Star Siege does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. The game works completely offline.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (for example whether sound is on) are saved only locally on your device inside the app's own storage. This data never leaves your device through the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. If you use Android's own device backup, Android may include this local data in your personal Google backup; that is handled by Google, not by us.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking

The app has no user accounts, contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and does not request internet access or any other permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Since no personal data is collected, no data from children is collected either.

Changes

If a future version adds online features (for example multiplayer), this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because we do not process personal data, there is normally nothing we hold about you. For questions contact novabytestudio.team@gmail.com.
